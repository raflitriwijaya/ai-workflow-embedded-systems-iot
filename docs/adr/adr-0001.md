# ADR-0001 — Adoption of ARB Charter and ADR Process

> The YAML frontmatter above is the machine-parseable source of truth and validates against
> [[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]] (rules V-ADR-01 … V-ADR-12). The sections below are the human-readable
> rendering of that frontmatter.

## Status

**DECIDED** — 2026-06-23. Decision class **STRATEGIC**; tier **CROSS-CUTTING**. Initiated and approved by the [[EMBEDDED_SYSTEMS_ARCHITECT_SKILL|Embedded Systems Architect]].

## Context

The project's governance layer defines an ADR (Architecture Decision Record) process in [[EMBEDDED_SYSTEMS_ARCHITECT_SKILL|Architect Skill]] §7 and an Architecture Review Board (ARB) charter in §7.Z, and a machine-parseable [[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]] exists in `docs/schemas/`. Two operational gaps — flagged in both the Review V3 audit and the Gemini Agentic Engineering review — blocked the autonomous-agent roadmap: (a) **no ADR storage location, numbering convention, or registry existed** (CLAUDE.md §6.1 `[NOT YET AVAILABLE]`); and (b) **the §7.Z ARB charter had no instantiated membership, quorum, or cadence** surfaced for operational use (CLAUDE.md §6.2 `[NOT YET AVAILABLE]`). Without these, the EN-1 (Emergent Property 1: Architect Singularity) mitigation could not function and STRATEGIC decisions had no recordable home.

## Problem Statement

How should the project instantiate ADR storage and the ARB so that architectural decisions are recorded, validated, indexed, and ratified through a distributed, auditable process consistent with the existing §7 / §7.Z governance?

## Alternatives Considered

- **Option A — Continue ad hoc.** Prose-only definitions, informal records. *Pro:* zero effort. *Con:* decisions stay unrecorded and un-auditable; violates Never-Silently-Deviate; leaves the EN-1 mitigation inert and the roadmap blocked.
- **Option B — Instantiate `docs/adr/` + the §7.Z ARB charter (chosen).** *Pro:* closes §6.1/§6.2 with concrete validatable artifacts; keeps §7.Z authoritative; enables the EN-1 mitigation and the roadmap; ADRs become machine-validatable. *Con:* new files must stay synchronized with §7.Z.
- **Option C — External ADR tool / separate registry.** *Pro:* off-the-shelf automation. *Con:* breaks the doc-as-code single-vault model; ADRs lose Obsidian linkage; introduces a new platform/security surface that would itself require an ADR.

## Decision

Adopt **Option B**. Establish `docs/adr/` as the canonical ADR repository with the lowercase `adr-NNNN.md` file convention and uppercase `ADR-NNNN` internal IDs (per [[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]] V-ADR-01), an append-only [[ADR_INDEX|ADR Index]], and an [[ADR_TEMPLATE|ADR Template]]. Concurrently adopt the [[ARB_CHARTER_INSTANTIATED|ARB Charter (Instantiated)]] as the operational surface of [[EMBEDDED_SYSTEMS_ARCHITECT_SKILL|Architect Skill]] §7.Z — with its membership, the 3-of-5 quorum (including at least one of Architect or Deputy), the bi-weekly 60-minute cadence, the ARB Decision Record template, and the per-release-cycle rotating-member policy.

## Rationale

The decision keeps the doc-as-code, single-vault governance model intact and re-uses the already-`final` definitions in §7, §7.Z, and the [[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]] — adding only the storage, numbering, index, and operational mechanics that were missing. It is the minimal change that closes the §6.1 / §6.2 gaps while preserving §7.Z as authoritative, and the structural prerequisite for the EN-1 mitigation and for distributing Tier 2–4 decision-making under MACP (Multi-Agent Coordination Protocol).

**Bootstrap authority note:** STRATEGIC ADRs ordinarily require ARB ratification ([[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]]). This foundational ADR establishes the ARB itself, so ARB ratification cannot precede it; it is ratified by the Architect under the unilateral authority over "the ADR repository and architecture governance" granted in [[EMBEDDED_SYSTEMS_ARCHITECT_SKILL|Architect Skill]] §2. The ARB's ratification role begins with the next STRATEGIC ADR. This ADR touches **no** reserved Decision Limit (no platform/protocol/OTA/security-baseline/resource-budget change), so no co-approval beyond the Architect is required. Named human ARB seat appointments and any change to a reserved class remain with the human Architect + CTO (Chief Technology Officer).

## Consequences

- `docs/adr/` is the canonical ADR repository; all future ADRs are authored from [[ADR_TEMPLATE|ADR Template]], validated against [[ADR_SCHEMA|ADR Schema]], and appended to [[ADR_INDEX|ADR Index]] §4.
- The [[ARB_CHARTER_INSTANTIATED|ARB Charter]] is in force; ARB Decision Records use the §7 ARB Decision Record template and are cross-referenced from the ADR Index when they would otherwise require an ADR.
- CLAUDE.md §6.1 and §6.2 `[NOT YET AVAILABLE]` markers are replaced with wikilinks to these artifacts.
- All roles are informed; the Architect maintains §7.Z↔instantiation synchronization under the [[EMBEDDED_SYSTEMS_ARCHITECT_SKILL|Architect Skill]] §3.6 drift-monitoring cadence.
